================================================================================
ABSTRACT
================================================================================
Microglia are activated in Huntington’s disease prior to symptom onset. Crapser et al. show that eliminating microglia from the R6/2 mouse model of Huntington’s disease reduces motor and cognitive impairments, accumulation of mutant huntingtin, and striatal volume loss. It also prevents the loss of perineuronal nets.
